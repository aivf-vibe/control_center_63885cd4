Frequently Asked Questions

How often is the data updated?

Portfolio data is updated in real-time with market data feeds. Risk metrics are recalculated every 15 minutes, while performance analytics are updated at market close.

What risk models are used?

We utilize multiple risk models including parametric VaR, Monte Carlo simulations, historical simulation, and stress testing scenarios based on major market events.

Can I customize the dashboard?

Yes, the dashboard is fully customizable. You can create custom views, set up personalized alerts, and configure which metrics are displayed prominently.

How is performance calculated?

Performance is calculated using time-weighted returns (TWR) methodology, adjusted for cash flows and fees. All returns are net of management and performance fees.
